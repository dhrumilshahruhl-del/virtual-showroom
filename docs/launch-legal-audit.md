# Launch Legal Audit

## Remediation update — 2026-10-16

This update supersedes conflicting technical statuses in the initial audit below; the detail below is retained as the original audit baseline. The production artifact is now generated by `npm run build` and includes only an explicit runtime allowlist; `dist/production-files.json` lists 70 files. It excludes docs, QA/audit/build scripts, source manifests, unused model exports, source-kit paths, and project notes. The six required Three.js r170 modules are self-hosted, and the Three.js MIT notice remains linked in the showroom and included in the output. Local production capture observed **zero third-party origins**, an empty cookie string, and zero local/session storage entries. The production scan found no forbidden paths, source maps, secret-like values, or absolute project/user paths. An upstream MeshoptDecoder copyright email was preserved as legitimate authorship metadata.

The 200 Water model status is **PENDING INTERNAL PUBLICATION CONFIRMATION**, based on the user's confirmation that RUHL employees developed the model. This does not establish publication permission or whether restricted source input was incorporated. Generic equipment images remain pending source and publication confirmation. Unsupported numerical impact graphics are retained but hidden by `SHOW_APPROVED_IMPACT_CLAIMS = false` in `src/config.js`; they remain NOT APPROVED. No HVAC engineering relationships were changed; see `system-flow-approval.md`.

Keyboard operation is implemented: the canvas is focusable and named, with arrow rotation/elevation, `+`/`=` and `-` zoom, and `R` reset. Keyboard input is ignored while System Flow owns the camera. Three complete six-step production cycles passed with a keyboard-operated cycle, stable renderer counts (76 geometries, 36 textures, 29 programs, 85 draw calls, 673,104 triangles), one reusable flow panel/popup/two leaders, and no browser console errors. Two exhaust plumes were visible in the exhaust step. The heat-pump dialog opened and closed by keyboard; reduced-motion flow removed camera transitions; switching tabs paused rendering and returning resumed it. Responsive widths 390, 768, 1024, 1440, and 720 CSS pixels showed no horizontal overflow. Heap samples varied from 190,237,972 to 214,261,512 to 211,561,256 bytes; this short run does not establish heap stability or prove absence of leaks. Production host logs, final policy match, screen-reader review, actual 200% browser-zoom review, and formal conformance assessment remain unknown/pending.

Audit date: 2026-10-16. This is an evidence-based repository/runtime audit, not legal advice or a guarantee of legal compliance. It covers the current checkout and a browser run of the static app on `http://localhost:4173/`; no public deployment URL or deployment configuration was present to compare.

## Executive status

**NOT READY** for public/commercial launch. The top blockers are the undocumented rights/source for the project-specific 200 Water building GLB and four equipment-image files, unsupported engineering and numerical claims in the public UI, and the absence of a production build/deployment allowlist to establish what is actually published. The city-context kit and named HDRI have favorable license evidence described below; they do not clear the other assets.

## Blockers

1. **BLOCKER — UNKNOWN PROVENANCE:** `assets/models/200-water-building.opt.glb` is the main project-specific 3D building/roof model. Its GLB metadata says only `glTF-Transform v4.4.2`; it provides no source, original author, license, client delivery terms, or web-publication authorization. Do not publish it until its source and commercial/public distribution rights are documented and RUHL/client authorization is confirmed.
2. **BLOCKER — UNKNOWN PROVENANCE:** `assets/equipments/ahu.png`, `assets/equipments/ahu-popup.webp`, `assets/equipments/heat_pump.png`, and `assets/equipments/heat_pump-popup.webp`. The WebP files are derivatives of the PNGs, so they inherit the same unknown rights. Confirm creator/source, license, and commercial web use. For any AI-generated image, record the generation/reference basis and have RUHL approve it.
3. **BLOCKER:** Public impact values have no approved engineering/energy source in the repository, and their current presentation does not say whether they are measured, modeled, projected, or estimated. Values in the live app: exhaust 74,780 CFM before and 21,480 CFM after; 53,300 CFM less exhaust; “284% negative” pressure to “Balanced / slight positive”; approximately 72% heat-recovery effectiveness; 26% ventilation energy reduction; 11.9M kBtu/year ventilation energy saved; 664 tCO₂e/year avoided. The 53,300 difference is arithmetic from the displayed CFM values, not independent evidence. Obtain the approved calculation/source and scope/status for every value or withhold the unsupported public values. The legacy extraction also contains 26,330 and 28,316 CFM; these were not found in the live UI and should stay out of production documentation unless independently approved.
4. **BLOCKER:** No production build script, bundler configuration, deployment workflow, hosting configuration, or production output (`dist`/`build`) exists in this checkout. The app loads source modules directly and Three.js from a CDN. Therefore the actual public artifact, headers, paths, and whether development/reference files are exposed cannot be established. Publish a reviewed production-only allowlist and inspect the resulting output at the intended public URL before launch. Do not serve the repository root by default.
5. **BLOCKER pending owner confirmation:** The exact RUHL Privacy Policy applicable to this showroom was not supplied or located at an authoritative RUHL policy URL during this audit. The runtime contacts jsDelivr, which receives the visitor's browser request and network IP address. RUHL must confirm that its published privacy notice covers this third-party delivery and the website host's ordinary request logs. The app itself does not set cookies or browser storage in the captured run.

## High-risk items

- **HIGH — client/project rights:** The project-specific 200 Water geometry, rooftop layout, system depiction, name/address, and any client-supplied source model require documented client/owner authorization for public web presentation and commercial association. Possession or a generic project folder does not prove public rights. Confirm no confidentiality or property-owner restriction applies.
- **HIGH — engineering review:** No approved engineering basis/schematic is present for the public System Flow statements that exhaust air passes through recovery before discharge, two air streams never mix, recovery circuits represent the displayed exchange, the heat pump is water-source, and it supplements heating/cooling before supply. The source model is explicitly anonymous and contains no hydraulic schematic or verified water-flow direction (`docs/system-flow.md`). An engineer/project owner should approve the narrative and visuals against source documents. The code correctly says energy pulses do not establish water-flow direction, but that does not verify all other flow assertions.
- **HIGH — factual status/endorsement:** The app uses declarative “Before/After,” “less,” “savings,” “avoided annually,” and equipment/system statements without a projected/estimated/measured status. Confirm which are proposed/projected versus installed/measured and add only approved qualifiers. Do not imply actual installed equipment or an endorsement by NYC, a manufacturer, the building owner, or a partner. The rooftop equipment illustrations are generic and unlabeled as manufacturer products, but the full roof model's status remains unknown.
- **HIGH — deployment exposure:** Current runtime does not import `docs/`, root extraction markdown, scripts, or unused source exports. However, no production allowlist/build exists; a static root/GitHub Pages-style publishing setup may make tracked docs, QA scripts, the city source manifest, unused original GLTFs/textures, and project extraction documents directly addressable. The old extraction documents contain extensive copied/reference implementation material and obsolete sample URLs. Keep source/reference material in the repository as requested, but publish only reviewed runtime files.
- **HIGH — privacy review:** jsDelivr's published policy says it may collect IP address, browser type/version, referrer domain, requested jsDelivr URLs, date/time, unique device identifiers and diagnostics; it says it uses these for delivery, download statistics, analysis/monitoring and security, while stating it does not associate the data with specific users or track individuals. Its policy also says it does not use cookies. Actual laws and consent obligations depend on RUHL's audience, deployment, and applicable jurisdictions. No consent banner requirement is inferred here; obtain a privacy professional's decision after RUHL confirms its policy and hosting logs. [jsDelivr privacy policy](https://github.com/jsdelivr/jsdelivr/blob/master/Privacy%20Policy.md), [subprocessors](https://github.com/jsdelivr/jsdelivr/blob/master/Sub-Processors.md).
- **HIGH — accessibility:** The main interactive canvas has an accessible name but is not keyboard-focusable and OrbitControls is configured for pointer interaction; no keyboard method for rotating/zooming the 3D scene was found. This excludes keyboard-only users from the primary interaction. Remediate with an accessible keyboard interaction or equivalent textual/stepwise alternative before representing the experience as accessible.
