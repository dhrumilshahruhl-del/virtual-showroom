# Asset rights register

## Remediation update — 2026-10-16

The repository-only descriptions immediately below are the initial audit baseline and are superseded only by the specific confirmed/remediated facts stated in this addendum.

The user confirms RUHL employees internally developed `assets/models/200-water-building.opt.glb` and the rooftop model. Current status is **PENDING INTERNAL PUBLICATION CONFIRMATION**, not unknown provenance. The current optimized GLB was generated with glTF-Transform v4.4.2. Public-use approval and whether restricted third-party/client/BIM/CAD source geometry was incorporated remain unverified; see [`model-provenance.md`](model-provenance.md). The earlier model row below records the initial repository-only evidence and is superseded by this confirmation for creator origin, not by a claim of ownership or publication rights.

The four current equipment images and WebP derivatives are in the production bundle and remain pending provenance/publication confirmation; details are in [`equipment-image-provenance.md`](equipment-image-provenance.md). City GLTF buffer references are rewritten by the production assembler to local basename-only paths. Their source-kit folders and manifests are not in `dist/`.

Audit date: 2026-10-16. Scope: the checked-out repository and the app served from its static root on port 4173. The app has no production build script or deployment configuration, so an actual public deployment artifact/host could not be identified. "Runtime" below means an asset referenced by the current `index.html`/JavaScript, confirmed against a browser request capture. It does not assert that this repository version is deployed publicly.

Risk labels follow the launch audit. **BLOCKER — UNKNOWN PROVENANCE** means the repository does not establish the source and publication rights; it is not a finding of infringement. A license found for one asset group does not transfer to another.
